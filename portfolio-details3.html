About the Project

Saheli is India's first AI-powered women's safety platform — a multimodal distress detection system that identifies danger from voice and text, triggers silent emergency alerts, and shares privacy-preserving real-time location with emergency contacts and authorities in under 3 seconds. Built as Lead Researcher & NLP Developer with a team of 4, Saheli is live, publicly accessible, and deployed for real-world use.

The system was selected from over 1,200 national nominations and recognised with the National Aegis Graham Bell Award 2025 by the Ministry of Electronics & IT, Government of India — presented by Shri Jayant Chaudhary, Minister of State for Electronics & IT. It also received a competitive AICTE Government of India research grant, and a research paper is currently under review at the Journal of Big Data (Springer, Q1).

Saheli integrates NLP-based keyword and sentiment analysis, a lightweight audio classification pipeline optimised for edge devices, and a secure cloud backend with a police-facing dashboard for real-time incident management. The platform prioritises discreet activation, low-latency alerting, and privacy-preserving data handling throughout.

Technical Architecture

Detection Layer

Local audio capture → amplitude + spectral feature extraction → ML keyword classifier → NLP sentiment analysis on text input

Alert Pipeline

Distress trigger → Haversine proximity sort → Twilio SMS/call dispatch → silent background operation (fake shutdown mode)

Authority Dashboard

Cloud backend ingestion → Google Maps real-time tracking → police dashboard with incident management and alert escalation
