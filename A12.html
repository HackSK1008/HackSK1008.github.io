About these Achievements

Across three tiers of competition — international, national, and state — Sumit consistently placed on the podium, demonstrating the ability to build and pitch compelling AI-driven solutions under pressure and in front of expert evaluation panels.

At the International Hackathon (PICT Pune), Sumit's team secured 1st Prize in the Healthcare Domain for an AI solution targeting posture correction and fitness optimisation — the foundation of what became FitMantra, a published Indian patent. At the PAN IIT National Hackathon, one of India's most competitive student innovation events, the team earned 3rd Rank for their AI-powered women's safety solution — an early version of Saheli, which later won the National Aegis Graham Bell Award. The State-Level Hackathon at MIT College, Barshi added a 2nd Place finish, further validating the technical depth and cross-domain versatility of Sumit's work.

Together these results span healthcare, fitness, and public safety — and reflect an ability to compete across domains, levels of competition, and team sizes while consistently delivering solutions with real deployment potential.
