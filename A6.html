About the Award

The Aegis Graham Bell Award is one of India's most prestigious national honours for technological innovation, presented annually by the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology, Government of India. It recognises groundbreaking contributions in AI, data science, and digital technology that demonstrate high social impact and technical excellence.

Sumit was recognised for Saheli — India's first AI-powered women's safety platform — which was selected from 1,200+ national nominations for its combination of social relevance, technical originality, and real-world deployment. The award was personally presented by Shri Jayant Chaudhary, Minister of State for Electronics & IT.

Saheli integrates multimodal distress detection (voice + text), privacy-preserving real-time location sharing, and a sub-3-second emergency alert pipeline. It is live and publicly accessible, and supported by a Government of India (AICTE) research grant.
