About This Honour

The "Pioneer of AI & Data Science" is the highest departmental honour bestowed by MMCOE, Pune at the culmination of a student's bachelor's journey. It is not awarded for a single achievement — it recognises the totality of a student's four-year contribution: their national and international representation of the department and college, their competition track record, their leadership in building student communities, their creation of socially impactful AI platforms, and their grassroots outreach beyond the campus.

Sumit was selected for this title for consistently expanding what the department stands for — taking its work to Slovakia, France, IIT campuses, and central government ministries; winning six competitions; mentoring the next generation of student innovators; and reaching communities that traditional technical education rarely touches.

The Five Pillars of This Recognition

1 · National & International Representation

Represented MMCOE and India at the International Hackathon in Slovakia, Europe (1 of 30 global finalist teams across 30 countries); competed at the PAN IIT National Hackathon (3rd Rank); presented research at the IIT Bombay AI Governance Symposium; and completed an international research internship at Avignon University, France — consistently carrying the department's name to stages it had never reached before.

2 · Six Hackathon & Competition Wins

Won 6 hackathons and competitions across international, national, and state levels — including 1st Prize (International, Healthcare Domain at PICT Pune), 3rd Rank (PAN IIT National), 2nd Rank (State-Level, MIT College Barshi), and multiple additional wins. These victories directly seeded Saheli and FitMantra — two live, nationally recognised AI platforms with government recognition and a published patent.

3 · Innovation Leadership — NEURA & IIC

As President of NEURA AI & Tech Club, collaborated with and led 50+ active members, organised multiple innovation-driven events and technical workshops for junior students, and transformed the club into an active research and hackathon community. As Innovation Coordinator for India's government-backed IIC, mentored 130+ student teams through structured design thinking, prototype development, and ethics-driven AI — helping dozens of teams compete and win at national levels.

4 · Social Impact — 3,000+ Students, 59 Rural Schools

Through NSS and community outreach programmes, personally engaged 3,000+ students across 59 rural schools in Maharashtra — teaching emerging technology concepts, AI literacy, and digital skills to communities that rarely receive this kind of exposure. Delivered dedicated counselling sessions on women's safety awareness — directly aligned with the mission of the Saheli platform and demonstrating that technical work can be a vehicle for community uplift and social equity.

5 · Social-Impact AI Platforms

Built two live, publicly accessible AI platforms that have become reference points for student-led innovation at MMCOE: Saheli — India's first AI-powered women's safety platform (National Aegis Graham Bell Award 2025, AICTE Government Research Grant, Springer Q1 under review) and FitMantra — an AI virtual gym trainer with a published Indian patent and 1st Prize at an international hackathon. Both platforms were built, deployed, and recognised within the four-year bachelor's journey.
