About this Achievement

Sumit was recognised as the Academic Topper of the AI & Data Science Department at MMCOE, Pune for three consecutive years, maintaining a CGPA above 9.0 across all six semesters — finishing with a final GPA of 9.85 / 10.0. This places him among the top performers in his cohort across one of Pune's leading engineering institutions.

The recognition reflects not just raw academic performance but a sustained command of challenging subjects spanning machine learning, deep learning, data structures, algorithms, database systems, and statistical computing. Sumit balanced this academic consistency with active research, three internships, multiple hackathon wins, and leadership of the college's AI & Tech Club — all simultaneously.

This track record of disciplined, top-tier academic performance has directly supported his admission to the MSE in Data Science program at the University of Pennsylvania, and demonstrates the analytical foundation required for graduate-level research and applied AI work.
