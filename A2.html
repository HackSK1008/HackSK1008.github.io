About the Event

The International Hackathon 2024, held in Slovakia, Europe, was a prestigious global competition that brought together 30 finalist teams from 30 countries to build innovative solutions for real-world challenges using AI, data science, and emerging technologies.

Sumit was selected as part of the sole team representing India — competing among the world's top student innovators in an intense multi-day event involving live demos, technical presentations, and evaluation by industry mentors and international judges. The team demonstrated their AI-driven solution on a global platform, receiving recognition for technical depth and cross-cultural collaboration.

This experience provided exposure to advanced simulation tools, global industry best practices, and produced research-grade prototypes — reinforcing Sumit's ability to compete and collaborate at the highest international level.
