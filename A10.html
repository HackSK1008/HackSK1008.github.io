About the Award

The Entrepreneurship Excellence Award 2025, presented by the ENSIN Forum & VIT Pune, recognises individuals who demonstrate exceptional leadership in fostering innovation, building entrepreneurial ecosystems, and inspiring the next generation of problem-solvers.

Sumit was honoured for his significant contributions to promoting entrepreneurship and innovation at the institutional and national level. As the President of NEURA — AI Club, he led a community of 50+ members, organising innovation-driven events and initiatives that encouraged students to develop real-world, impact-oriented solutions.

In his role as Innovation Coordinator at the Institute Innovation Council (Government of India), he mentored over 130 student teams for more than a year, guiding them through design thinking, problem identification, and solution development. Several of these teams achieved recognition at national and international platforms, reflecting the effectiveness of his mentorship and ecosystem-building efforts.

Beyond campus, Sumit has been invited to deliver sessions on innovation and entrepreneurship at prestigious platforms including IIT Bombay and Infosys India, collectively engaging 1500+ students and fostering a culture of creativity, leadership, and entrepreneurial thinking.

This recognition highlights his ability to not only build impactful AI solutions but also empower others to innovate — demonstrating leadership, mentorship, and a sustained commitment to driving real-world change through entrepreneurship.
