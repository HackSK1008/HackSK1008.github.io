Building AI that solves real problems

I'm a final-year B.E. student in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Savitribai Phule Pune University (MMCOE) with a GPA of 9.85/10, and I'll be joining the MSE in Data Science program at the University of Pennsylvania in Fall 2025. My work spans applied AI end-to-end from NLP and computer vision to time-series forecasting and network security research.

I led Saheli, India's first AI-powered women's safety platform, which earned the National Aegis Graham Bell Award 2025 from the Ministry of Electronics & IT and secured a competitive Government of India (AICTE) research grant. I also engineered FitMantra, an AI virtual gym trainer with a published Indian patent (App. No. 202421004780) that achieved a ~60% reduction in posture errors during testing.

I also represented India at the International Hackathon 2024 in Slovakia, Europe — selected among 30 global finalist teams across 30 countries.

Outside engineering, I mentored 130+ student innovators as Innovation Coordinator for India's government-backed IIC, and served as President of NEURA, my college's AI & Tech Club. I believe technology is most powerful when it is scalable, ethical, and built with the communities it serves.
